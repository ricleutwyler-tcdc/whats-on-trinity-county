About What's On

A collaboration of the Trinity County Community Development Corporation and The Trinity Journal, and part of TCDC’s work to build a community of place in Trinity County.

Sources & how this was built

Events are drawn from The Trinity Journal's weekly Calendar of Events (compiled and distributed by the Trinity County Chamber) and confirmed against the Journal or the event host's own site; each card's “More info” link points to that source, or to the Journal calendar for recurring items without a dedicated page. This is a rolling look-ahead of about two weeks. Weather is a separate forecast overlay shown only for events within about ten days (approximate for the smaller towns); beyond that it reads “forecast closer to the date.” Confirm times and prices via “More info” before you go; “Directions” opens Google Maps to the venue.

The page follows the Journal's calendar; it doesn't replace it.
