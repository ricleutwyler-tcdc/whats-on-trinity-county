Get your event noticed
You're holding an event. Let the public know what's going on, the where and when (and how much), by sending us your information.
Placing your event in The Trinity Journal's Calendar of Events does much more than get you into the weekly newspaper. The calendar is uploaded to two locations (listing and calendar versions) on our website, www.trinityjournal.com, and also shared with the Trinity County and Weaverville chambers of commerce and their respective websites, www.VisitTrinity.com and www.weavervilleca.org, as well as the Trinity County Visitor Center, 661 Main St., Weaverville. It's also sent to UpState California.
Submit calendar items -- including time, date, location, admission fees, etc. -- to the Journal at our office, 500 Main St., Weaverville, CA 96093; or, preferably, via e-mail to editor@trinityjournal.com.
